Paste configuration

Paste show running-config or show access-lists output with the interface Vlan and ACL blocks, in any order. Only ip access-group attaches an ACL.

How results are worked out

Scope. One switch at a time. Everything stays in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded or saved.

Read from the paste. IPv4 standard and extended ACLs (named and numbered), sequence order and hit counters, any wildcard mask, host/CIDR/any, TCP/UDP ports (numbers, common names, eq lists, ranges), ICMP types and codes, and established.

A cell is traffic from the source VLAN to the destination, through the source IN ACL, then the destination OUT ACL. Every address and port is checked.

Results. Allowed: the ACLs permit all of it. Blocked: they permit none of it. With exceptions: part is permitted; the icons say what. No ACL: no ACL applies in either direction. Needs input: something missing or unsupported (e.g. an undefined ACL) could change the answer.

Columns. Your VLANs, plus networks named in deny rules (Deny targets) and permit rules (Other networks). Other IPv4 is every address outside your VLANs and those networks.

Not modeled. Routing between VRFs, NAT, firewalls, return traffic, fragments and traffic sent by the switch itself.

Reference: Cisco — Configure IP Access Lists.
